## 5. Visual Design Direction
* **Vibe:** Premium industrial-tech aesthetic. Sell confidence and expertise first, technology second.
* **Base Palette:** Dark Navy / Charcoal, White, Light Gray.
* **Accents (Use sparingly for differentiation):**
  * CCTV → Blue
  * Fire → Red/Orange
  * Solar → Yellow/Green
  * Geyser → Warm Orange

## 6. Marketing & Tracking (UTM/GCLID)
* Ignore `?utm_source=...` and `&gclid=EALw_wcB` in URL structures. These are purely for Google Ads/Analytics tracking and have no bearing on how the website routes should be built. Use clean URLs like `/services/solar`.
